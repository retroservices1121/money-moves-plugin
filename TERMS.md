# Money Moves Terms of Use

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Money Moves is an informational personal-finance workflow published by Retro Services for use inside ChatGPT.

## Informational use

Money Moves helps organize financial context, track user-defined limits, identify meaningful changes, and present possible financial actions for the user's consideration.

Money Moves does not provide banking, brokerage, investment-management, tax-preparation, legal, or fiduciary services.

## User decisions

Users remain responsible for financial decisions and for verifying balances, transactions, bills, due dates, account terms, and other important information with the relevant financial institution or provider.

## No money movement

The skills-only version of Money Moves does not initiate transfers, payments, purchases, trades, account openings, account closures, credit applications, or changes to automatic payments.

## Third-party services

Money Moves may rely on financial context made available through ChatGPT or connected services. Those services are governed by their own terms and privacy policies.

## Availability

Features may change, be unavailable, or depend on ChatGPT capabilities and connected services.
