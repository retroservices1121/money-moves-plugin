# Money Moves Privacy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

Money Moves v0.2 is a skills-only ChatGPT plugin. The published plugin package contains instructions that help ChatGPT organize and interpret financial context already available to the user in ChatGPT.

## What Money Moves receives

The skills-only plugin does not operate a separate Money Moves account service, does not require a separate bank connection, and does not send the user's bank data to a Money Moves server.

Financial information used by ChatGPT remains subject to the user's ChatGPT settings and the terms and privacy practices of OpenAI and any connected financial-data providers.

## What Retro Services stores

For the skills-only release, Retro Services does not receive or store users' bank credentials, bank access tokens, account balances, transaction histories, card numbers, or financial account identifiers through the plugin.

## Permissions

Money Moves is informational and advisory. It does not move money, pay bills, trade assets, open or close accounts, or change payment settings.

## Changes

If a future version adds an external Money Moves service, MCP server, account system, or other data processing, this policy will be updated before that functionality is released.
