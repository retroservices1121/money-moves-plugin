# Money Moves Support

For help with Money Moves, report an issue at:

https://github.com/retroservices1121/money-moves-plugin/issues

When reporting a problem, do not include bank passwords, PINs, full card numbers, CVVs, one-time codes, recovery codes, or other authentication secrets.

For problems involving ChatGPT Finances connections or ChatGPT account access, use OpenAI support because those connections are not operated by Money Moves.
